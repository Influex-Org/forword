Meet ForWord

Dmitriy Kozlov

Chief Expression Officer. Artist. Entrepreneur. Iconic Wanderer.

Dmitriy Kozlov has spent over a decade building Iconic Brands and World-Class Websites for some of the most recognized names in the entrepreneurial world. Ryan Deiss. Mike Dillard. Frank Kern. Joe Polish. Dan Lok. From Virgin Unite to legacy-building billionaires, his work has helped multidimensional creators translate their soul essence into a brand the world can feel.

As Chief Expression Officer at Influex and co-creator of Signature Soul Brand, a methodology where you discover, define, and design your brand from the depth of your soul, Dmitriy lives at the intersection of poetry and strategy, artistry and authority.

Under the stage name ForWord, he moves audiences through spoken word, turning the entrepreneurial journey into art.

He lives passionately in Austin, TX with his partner in life, love, business, and music, Carla Samson, their two brilliant dogs, and their loving cat, Torrey.
